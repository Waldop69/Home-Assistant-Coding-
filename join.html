Faith. Family. Community. Life.

Who Can Join?

The Knights of Columbus is the world's largest Catholic fraternal service organization. Membership is open to practical (practicing) Catholic men age 18 or older who are in union with the Holy See. Since 2018, an entire family can be part of the Knights of Columbus family, with wives and daughters welcome to participate in Columbiettes and other auxiliary programs (update this line to match your council's local auxiliary, if any).

Why Join Council 1016?

Faith

Grow in your Catholic faith alongside other men committed to living it out.

Community

Support parish events, local charities, and families in need.

Brotherhood

Build lifelong friendships with men who share your values.

Family Benefits

Access to the Knights of Columbus insurance and financial programs for your family.

How to Join

  1. Join online for free at kofc.org/join — use Council # 1016 when prompted so you're placed directly in our council.
  2. Or, contact our Membership Director directly and we'll walk you through the process in person.
  3. Attend a council meeting or event as a guest to meet the Brother Knights before joining — all are welcome.
