The Patriotic Degree — Faith and Patriotism

What Is the Fourth Degree?

The Fourth Degree is the patriotic degree of the Knights of Columbus, open to Knights in good standing who have completed the first three degrees. Fourth Degree Knights, often recognized by their ceremonial regalia, emphasize patriotism and love of country alongside faith and service. Fourth Degree members belong to a local Assembly rather than a council.
