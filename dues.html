Setting This Up (One-Time)

  1. The council needs a PayPal account (Business account recommended so it's tied to the council, not a personal member) — free to create, only a per-transaction fee when money is received.
  2. Get the email address associated with that PayPal account and paste it into the business field in the button code above.
  3. Set the amount field to match current annual dues.
  4. Optional but recommended: once the site is live on your real domain, update return/cancel_return to point at a simple "Thank you" page and back to this page, so members land somewhere sensible after paying.
  5. Test it yourself with a small real payment (or PayPal's sandbox/test mode) before announcing it to members.

Prefer Stripe or Square instead of PayPal? Both offer free hosted "Payment Links" you create in their dashboard (no coding) — just replace the PayPal form above with a plain link (<a class="btn btn-gold" href="YOUR_STRIPE_OR_SQUARE_LINK">Pay Dues →</a>) pointing at that payment link. Let your webmaster know which processor you'd like and this can be swapped in.
