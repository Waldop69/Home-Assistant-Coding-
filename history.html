The Knights of Columbus

The Knights of Columbus was founded in 1882 in New Haven, Connecticut, by Father Michael J. McGivney, a young parish priest who wanted to provide financial aid and support to Catholic immigrant families, especially widows and orphans, at a time when Catholics faced significant discrimination in America. What began as a small parish-based mutual benefit society has grown into the world's largest Catholic fraternal service organization, with millions of members worldwide.

Father McGivney was beatified by the Catholic Church in 2020, recognizing his legacy of charity and service that continues through the Knights of Columbus today.

The order is built on four core principles: Charity, Unity, Fraternity, and Patriotism.
